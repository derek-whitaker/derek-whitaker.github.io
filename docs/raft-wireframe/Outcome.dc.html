Strong synthesis led to a confident pivot. Weeks of intense, rigorous research and synthesis gave us confidence to redirect the client from a submission portal to a coordination tool, providing them greater value and solving more of their problems.
Have an engineer on the team from the beginning. We started without one, and de-risked designs through regular check-ins with the client’s lead developer. Once we had integrated developers towards the end of the project, we gained a lot of efficiencies and improved our technical de-risking.
