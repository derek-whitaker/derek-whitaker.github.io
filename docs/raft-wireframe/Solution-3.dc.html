Solving for non-linear workflows

The challenge
  • The workflows are fluid depending on the request in question. One request may need a review from a weapons specialist, another from the office of general counsel.
  • People also often change positions, so it can’t be assumed that last year’s point of contact is still this year’s.
Our approach
  1. 1Allow for flexible routing of funding requests. Workflows are not automated or set in stone; requests can be sent to any user at any time.
  2. 2Workflow Group managers can create suggestions that guide users on who to send requests to.

Solving for form confusion

The challenge
  • Confusion filling out requests led to poor-quality requests and rework.
Our approach
  1. 1Embed clear, validated guidance contextually into the tool.
  2. 2Validate guidance with real submitters to ensure it’s easy to understand.

Solving for historical record

The challenge
  • Both implementing agencies and the client need to reference past-year submissions, to better draft current-year submissions and make better adjudication decisions, respectively.
  • This record needs to respect the same visibility constraints as current-year requests.
Our approach
  1. 1A searchable database of prior-year submissions, limited to the ones your Community has visibility on.
  2. 2This same system of record powers an analytics dashboard that shows high-level statistics and trend analysis.

Launched December 2021

As we finalized designs, we had an important shift in the delivery method. We had originally planned to work with a government development shop to build and deploy the tool. However, its workload was so high that it was unclear when they would be able to begin work on it. Drawing on the strong working relationship we’d built, we pitched the client on taking on development ourselves. They agreed and funded our team to handle delivery, and the tool launched in December 2021.
